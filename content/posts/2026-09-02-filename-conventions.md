## Name a file with a date

`2026-09-02-filename-conventions.md`

That's how this very post gets its slug (`filename-conventions`) and its date
(`2026-09-02`) without writing a single line of frontmatter beyond the title.

## Drafting

Set `published: false` in frontmatter to keep a post out of the list and the
sitemap while you write it.
